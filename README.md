# Missori

## A BreakTalk product

Missori is a destination assistant for travelers and local businesses. It helps people discover local places, explore communities, and organize travel information.

## Product highlights

* Local place discovery and destination guidance
* Conversational travel assistance
* Personal itineraries and saved travel information
* Traveler and business perspectives
* Selected on-device features for use with limited connectivity

Feature availability depends on the released version, location, connectivity and service configuration. A directory listing does not establish a partnership or business endorsement.
