# Missori (미소리)

**Missori is an AI partner connecting people and businesses.**
미소리는 사람과 비즈니스를 연결하는 AI 파트너입니다.

Time and belonging for people. Local shops found and remembered. A neighbourhood that grows together.

Missori is a BreakTalk product, a local tourism, lifestyle and business platform built and tested in Sejong, South Korea. Its name joins 미소 (smile) and 소리 (sound): the sound that brings a smile.

## The problem: no time to belong, no time to be found

People are short on time and want to belong, yet they go to the chains they already know, while good local shops, facing fierce competition with no time to spare, get walked past. In between, the neighbourhood loses its community. This is not a foreigners-only problem; it is part of everyone's everyday life.

| Figure | What it measures in Korea | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 65.5% | Working people who say they are usually short of time | Statistics Korea, 2024 Time Use Survey |
| 56.6% | People who spend their leisure alone; about 1 in 5 feel lonely | 2025 National Leisure Activity Survey; 2024 Social Integration Survey |
| 61.0% | Small business owners who name competition as their top difficulty; only 27.2% use any digital tool | 2024 Small Business Survey |
| 37.1% | Owners who rest 7 days a month or fewer | KBIZ, 2024 small business work and life survey |

## The answer: a good day for people, regulars for shops

- A person tells Missori their free hours, taste, budget and company (alone, as two, family, older adults). Missori builds the day or weekend from real places nearby and shows it on a map, where each stop can be kept or swapped.
- A message to a shop is translated, translated back and approved by the person before it is sent.
- Shops turn visitors into regulars (단골) with a QR check-in, stamps and come-back messages that the owner approves.
- A guest at one shop walks on to the next. People save time and belong; local shops grow together.

## Who it is for

- **People:** residents of every age, Korean and foreign, including people living alone and older adults, and the visitors that hotels, agencies and platforms host.
- **Local businesses:** new customers who come back.
- **Partners:** travel agencies, small hotel chains, Airbnb hosts and clubs from 2027, then travel platforms and hotel groups, who offer Missori to their own guests.
- **Cities and tourism bodies:** which experiences people enjoy, shown as totals that identify no one.

## How it compares: partners list places, Missori plans the whole day

| What people need | Maps (NAVER, Kakao, Google) | Booking platforms | Chat assistants | Missori |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A whole day around my time, diet and budget | One place at a time | One booking at a time | Drafts a day; 0.6% of test plans valid | Real places on a map you edit |
| Help in my language, all day | Interface only | Booking support | Yes, without local checks | Six languages, voice or text |
| Checked before it reaches a shop | No | No | No | Translated back, with a checklist |
| The shop sees the visit and can bring people back | No | No | No | QR membership and stamps |

Partners bring the visitor and keep booking and payment; Missori plans the day and proves the visit. The 0.6% is the share of GPT-4 Turbo plans that met every constraint in the TravelPlanner benchmark (ICML 2024).

## Who pays

People get Missori through the businesses that serve them, and people without a partner can subscribe. Prices are proposals that the pilot will test. The full price list is shared with programmes and investors.

| Plan | Who pays | How it is paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missori: belonging and time (the day planner, checked chat and guides) | A partner or city, for the people they serve | Free to the person |
| Missori Platinum: plus a bit of exclusivity, perks from local partners, kept by going out twice a month | People directly, or businesses under their own name | Monthly membership |
| Shop Plus: a customer list, come-back messages and reports | Small shops | Monthly subscription |
| Business edition | Agencies, small hotel chains, Airbnb hosts and clubs; then platforms and hotel groups | Setup fee plus a monthly fee per active user |
| Region edition, with visit reports | Cities, districts, tourism bodies, festivals | Setup fee plus a monthly fee per active user |

Levels are earned by good days, never bought: Guest, Regular (단골), Local and Host.

## The plan: not for sale yet, we test first

- **October to November 2026:** a survey of 30 local shop owners in Sejong and 20 interviews.
- **December 2026:** a first free test with 5 to 10 shops and about 30 people.
- **January to March 2027:** a street pilot in Naseong-dong, Sejong, with 30 to 50 shops and their customers.
- **End of March 2027, two gates before any sale:** market fit with local businesses (20 or more shops using it every week, 10 or more owners ready to pay for Shop Plus, 30% of visitors back within 60 days) and simple and easy to use (an owner sets up in 10 minutes, a first-time user plans a day in 2 minutes, 8 of 10 testers, older adults included, need no help).
- **2027 to 2030:** prove it in Sejong, then all of Chungcheong in 2028, six regions in 2029, and the whole country, then abroad, in 2030.

These are targets, not results.

## Trust by design

- Nothing reaches a shop before the person approves it.
- Missori takes no booking or order payments. Booking and payment stay with partners such as NAVER and Toss.
- Route data is never sold, and each business sees only its own guests who chose to join it.
- Cities see visits as totals that identify no one.
