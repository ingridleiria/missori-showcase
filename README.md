# Missori (미소리)

Missori is an AI partner that turns free time into local experiences, connecting people and businesses. It is a BreakTalk product, a local tourism, lifestyle and business platform built and tested in Sejong, South Korea.

미소리는 여가 시간을 지역 경험으로 바꾸고, 사람과 비즈니스를 연결하는 AI 파트너입니다.

## Why it exists

People are short on time and want to belong somewhere, and good local shops compete alone, with little time to be found. Missori sits between the two. It gives people a good day out through real places nearby, in their own language, and it gives shops visitors who come back. Missori is for everyone: residents and visitors, Korean and foreign, families, people on their own and older adults.

## What works today

| Feature | For whom | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day planner with real places nearby and a map where every stop can be kept or swapped | People | Public prototype |
| Checked chat with shops: each message is translated, translated back and approved before it is sent | People and shops | Public prototype |
| Voice conversation without typing, a café kiosk guide and everyday guides | People, older adults, newcomers | Public prototype |
| Six interface languages: Korean, English, Portuguese, Spanish, Chinese and Vietnamese | Everyone | Public prototype |
| Free shop page, QR membership, stamps and points | Shops | Public prototype |
| iPhone app | Everyone | In testing |
| Editions for hotels, agencies and platforms, and region editions with visit reports | Businesses and cities | Planned for 2027 |

The directory behind the planner starts with 15,816 Sejong businesses from public records.

## Where it is now

Missori is free and not for sale. A survey of local shop owners in Sejong runs until 30 November 2026, a first free test with 5 to 10 shops follows in December, and a street pilot in Naseong-dong, Sejong, runs from January to March 2027. Paid services start only after the pilot shows market fit with local businesses and proves easy to use.

## Trust by design

- Nothing reaches a shop before the person approves it.
- Missori takes no payments. Booking and payment stay with partners such as NAVER and Toss.
- Route data is never sold, and each shop sees only the guests who chose to join it.
